Melkam Kalat Poster: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Melkam Kalat Poster is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @melkamkalat.

  • What we access: the account's basic profile (open ID, display name) and permission to upload content to that account's drafts.
  • What we store: an access token, kept encrypted as a GitHub Actions secret, and a log of which quote images were sent and when.
  • What we share: nothing. No data is sold or shared with third parties. Quote images are published at a public URL only so TikTok can download them.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
